# Discount missing on an exact $100.00 subtotal

I expect a 10% discount once the merchandise subtotal reaches 10000 cents. The discount is 10% of the subtotal, rounded down to the nearest cent. An order whose subtotal is exactly 10000 cents comes back with a discount of 0 cents.

Example: a subtotal of 9999 cents should get no discount. A subtotal of 10000 cents should get a discount of 1000 cents. A subtotal of 10001 cents should get a discount of 1000 cents.
